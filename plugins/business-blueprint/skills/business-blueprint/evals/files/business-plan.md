# My business plan

I want to help small business owners use AI so they save time. There are lakhs of small
businesses in Punjab and most of them do not use any AI. I will offer a monthly service,
maybe Rs 2,000 to Rs 5,000 a month depending on the work, where I set up ChatGPT and a
few automations for them and support them on WhatsApp.

I can work full time on this. I have about Rs 50,000 saved. I know AI tools well because
I have used them daily for a year and I did a course. Marketing will be through Instagram
and word of mouth.

The main risk is whether they will pay monthly.

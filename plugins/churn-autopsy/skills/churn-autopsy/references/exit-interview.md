# The exit interview: five questions

The stated reason column is the only column the customer fills in. Where it is
[PENDING], nothing in the sheet can replace it. These five questions fill it. The agent
drafts them in the owner's voice; the owner sends them; the answers come back verbatim
into NOTES.

## The five

1. What did you want to happen when you signed up with us?
2. What actually happened in the first month?
3. What was the moment you decided to stop?
4. What would have had to be true for you to stay?
5. Where did you go instead, and what are you paying there?

Ask about what happened, never about how they feel. "Were you happy with us" produces
politeness. "What was the moment you decided" produces a date and a reason.

## How they are sent

- By the owner, in the owner's name, on the channel the customer used with the business.
  A customer who lived on WhatsApp will not answer an email survey.
- One message, five questions, no form, no rating scale, no discount attached. A discount
  attached to a question buys a kind answer and kills the data.
- Within 30 days of leaving where possible. After 90 days the reason has been rewritten
  by memory.
- To every row where the stated reason is [PENDING], not to a sample. Three answers can
  change a ranking.

## How the answers are used

- Pasted into NOTES exactly as they came, typos included, with the row id.
- Each answer becomes the stated reason for its row, in the customer's words.
- Then the five suspects are scored again on the rows that changed. Say which rows moved
  and whether the ranking moved with them.
- A customer who does not answer is a fact too: the stated reason stays [PENDING] and
  the last contact column records the attempt.

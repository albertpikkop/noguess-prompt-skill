# The product segment: about 90 seconds, five beats

Every product in the show gets the same five beats, in the same order, so the host never
has to think about what comes next and the viewer always knows when the buying part is
coming. Write one segment per product into the plan. Words in [brackets] are where the
seller's own facts go; if the seller has not given a fact, the bracket becomes
[PENDING: what is needed] and the beat is said without it. Lines without a named source
are this kit's advice.

## The five beats

| Beat | About | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Who it is for | 10 seconds | Names the buyer, so the right people lean in and the wrong ones do not waste the desk's time |
| 2. Show it | 30 seconds | Does the thing a photo cannot: drape, pour, fold, cut, fill, wear, switch on |
| 3. Prove it | 15 seconds | Only proof the seller actually has. None is allowed; invented is not |
| 4. The price | 15 seconds | The price and the delivery charge together, and the show-only price only if there is one |
| 5. How to buy | 20 seconds | The code, the WhatsApp step, and the real count left |

## The shape, with brackets

```text
PRODUCT [code]: [name as the seller calls it]   role: [opener / bestseller / profit / anchor / closer]

1. WHO IT IS FOR
   "Ye un ke liye hai jo [the buyer and the moment, in the seller's words]."

2. SHOW IT
   [what the host does with it on camera, step by step]
   [the one detail to bring close to the lens]

3. PROVE IT
   [a customer's words, read with their permission]  or
   [a test done on camera]  or
   [PENDING: what proof you have]

4. THE PRICE
   "[price], plus [delivery charge] delivery."
   Show-only price, only if there is one: "[show price] until [exact end time]."

5. HOW TO BUY
   "Comment [code]. [Desk person's name] aapko WhatsApp karenge.
    Payment aane pe order confirm. Abhi [real count] bache hain."

Then: read the comments, answer the two most asked questions, say the count again, next product.
```

## Rules for every segment

- **One product at a time.** Never two on screen while one is being sold.
- **The same buying words every time,** so viewers learn the ritual.
- **Say the count as a number, only as the desk reports it.** Never round it down to sound
  scarce; see `honest-urgency.md`.
- **Answer questions by name.** "[viewer's name] puch rahi hain ki size kya hai." A buyer who hears
  their own name stays.
- **If a product has no proof, do not fill the beat.** Show it longer instead. "This is
  new, you are the first to see it" may be said only if the seller told you it is new.
- **Say the running order at the start and every 10 minutes.** People arrive late.

## Recorded, not live

A video that stays up after the show (a practice video before the first show, the
bestseller video, a clip cut from the live) drops two things that are true only during
the live: the count left and any show-only price. Beat 5 in a recording is the code and
how to buy, with no count. It ends with the show's day and time once the Live screen says
yes; until then, "Follow kar lijiye, live ka din yahin bataenge." A count that was true on
Saturday and is still on screen on Tuesday is false scarcity.

## Before the show: the rehearsal

Say every segment out loud with a timer, once, the day before. A segment that runs past
two minutes has too much in beat 2 or beat 3; cut there, never in beats 4 and 5.

## What never goes in a segment

- A fabric, a weight, an ingredient, a size, a certificate, a review or a price the
  seller did not give.
- A comparison with a named competitor.
- Anything about income, savings the buyer will make, or results the buyer will get,
  unless the seller has proof and says where it comes from.

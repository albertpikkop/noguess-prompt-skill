# The order desk: from comment to paid order

On Instagram and Facebook there is no buy button inside a live. The order desk is the
buy button, run by hand. It is the difference between a show people enjoy and a show
people buy from.

## Who

A second person, on a second phone, watching the live as a viewer and working WhatsApp
at the same time. With no second person, the host pauses for 60 seconds after each
product to note the codes, and does the desk's work within one hour after the show.

## The flow

```text
0. Before the first product, the host says the rule on air: "[code] ke [count] piece hain.
   Pehle comment ko pehla mauka. Payment [time] tak."
1. The buyer comments the product code (S1, S2, ...) on the live.
2. The desk notes: code, the buyer's name or handle, the time. The order of comments is the
   order of chances; more comments than pieces means a waiting line, said on air.
3. The desk asks the buyer for their WhatsApp number in a DM, or the buyer messages the
   business number shown on screen. Never ask for a number in the public comments.
4. The desk sends message A (below): the item, the price, the delivery charge, how to pay,
   and the pay-by time. The pay-by time is a clock time at least two hours after the last
   message A will have gone out (this kit's advice).
5. The buyer pays by UPI or the payment link.
6. The desk checks that the money shows in the seller's own bank or UPI app, marks the
   order paid, and sends message B. A screenshot is not payment.
7. The desk tells the host the new count. The host says it on air.
8. If two people paid for the last piece, refund the second the same day and tell them why.
9. Within one hour after the show: message B to anyone who paid after the live ended.
10. About 30 minutes before each buyer's pay-by time: message C, once, if they have not
   paid. One reminder, not more.
11. At the pay-by time, an unpaid claim passes to the next in line, who gets message A with
   their own pay-by time, also at least two hours away.
```

An order is confirmed when the money is seen. A comment is interest, not an order.

## The desk's sheet

One row per claim. Keep it for every show; it is also the record that every count said
on air was true.

| time | code | buyer | WhatsApp | price | delivery | paid (yes/no) | count left after |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## The messages

Written in the plan before the show, in the seller's language, with their real numbers.
Brackets are the seller's facts; a missing fact is [PENDING].

```text
A. THE DETAILS
Namaste [name], [shop name] se. Aapne live mein [code] comment kiya.
[product]: [price], plus [delivery charge] delivery. Total [total].
Payment: UPI [UPI ID] ya ye link: [payment link]. [pay-by time] tak.
Payment aane pe order confirm hoga. [return or exchange rule, one line].

B. PAID
Dhanyavaad [name]. [total] mil gaya. [product] [dispatch date] tak dispatch hoga.
[Tracking yahin bhejenge / pickup: place and time].

C. ONE REMINDER, ONLY IF UNPAID
[name], [product] aapke liye [time] tak rakha hai. Nahi chahiye to bas bata dijiye,
koi baat nahi.
```

Message C is the only reminder. After it, silence. A second or third chase is nagging,
and nagging is on the dark patterns list in `honest-urgency.md`.

## Payment

- **UPI or a payment link** is the default. The buyer pays before dispatch.
- **Payments inside the WhatsApp chat** exist for businesses in India on the WhatsApp
  Business Platform, with Razorpay and PayU as partners (announced 19 September 2023). On
  the free WhatsApp Business app, the simple way is a UPI ID, a QR code or a payment link.
  Either works for a first show.
- **Cash on delivery** is the seller's call. Label it as an assumption to test against
  their own orders: [Assumption: cash on delivery orders come back more often than
  prepaid orders here. Check the last 20.] Never quote an industry number for it.
- **The desk never asks a buyer for an OTP, a card PIN, a UPI PIN or a password,** and says so in the
  trust kit, together with the one business number the seller messages from.
- **Paid means credited:** the money shows in the seller's own bank or UPI app. Never
  dispatch on a screenshot.
- **The list:** ask each buyer to save the business number, so the next show's
  announcement reaches them.

## When stock runs out

Say it on air with the count: "[code] khatam, sab [total] bik gaye." Offer another product only if
the buyer asks, at its own price, never as if it were the same product. See bait and
switch in `honest-urgency.md`.
